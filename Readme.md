## 🎯 Business Problem

> *"Which shipping modes, regions, and product categories 
> are causing the most delivery delays — 
> and how much profit is being lost as a result?"*

In today's competitive e-commerce landscape, late deliveries 
directly impact customer satisfaction and company revenue. 
This project dives deep into a real-world supply chain dataset 
to identify bottlenecks and provide actionable business insights.

## 🔍 Key Findings

| Metric | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| 📦 Total Orders Analyzed | 180,000+ |
| 🚚 Worst Shipping Mode | Standard Class |
| 🌍 Most Delayed Region | Central America |
| 📉 Late Delivery Impact | Lower avg profit per order |
| ⚠️ Most Delayed Category | Golf Bags & Carts |

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1.🚫 Avoid Standard Class shipping for high-value orders
2.🌍 Investigate supplier reliability in high-delay regions
3.📦 Prioritize top 10 delayed product categories
4.💰 Focus on on-time delivery to protect profit margins
